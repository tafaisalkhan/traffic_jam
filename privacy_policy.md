# City Rescue Puzzle Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

City Rescue Puzzle saves level progress, stars, coins, best moves, sound and graphics preferences, selected vehicle colour, and ad removal status in local app storage on your device. City Rescue Puzzle does not run its own account or game data server.

## Advertising

When advertising is enabled in an Android release build, Google AdMob may serve banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. After you continue past the in-app privacy notice, AdMob may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad views, interactions, and diagnostics under [Google's advertising policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). When ads are unavailable or disabled, no ads are shown.

## Purchases

When Google Play purchases are enabled in a release build, Google Play handles payment and purchase verification for Remove Ads, coin packs, and support purchases. City Rescue Puzzle stores only the resulting game entitlement or coin balance locally and does not receive payment card details.

## Data control

You can erase locally saved game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Google's handling of ad and purchase data follows its own retention and deletion policies.

City Rescue Puzzle does not ask players to create an account or enter personal information. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of ads and purchases.

The in-app notice explains data use. It is not a choice about personalized advertising.
